## Site design notes
- Page background: light lavender/periwinkle gray (~#DCDCE6-ish; sample from screenshot).
- Content area: white.
- Header: "XKCDB" wordmark (gray), xkcd-style stick figures (see xkcdb-header-figures.png),
  tagline: "That's what SHE said. We have logs."
- Nav links: About | Submit | New | Top (Recent) | Random >0 — dark blue links.
- Search box + "Go" button, RSS icon (orange), banana icon, "$" icon on the right.
- Quote headers: #number in blue, green up-arrow, (+up/-down) vote counts, red down-arrow.
- Quote body: monospace font, black on white.
- EXCLUDE: the green "Hi! Just so you know, you can click on the $ up there..."
  donation box (top right, xkcdb-original.png).
