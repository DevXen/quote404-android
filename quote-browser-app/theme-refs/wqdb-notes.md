# wqdb.org (WormsQuoteDatabase) theme reference (from Clint's screenshot, 2026-09-15)

Source: quote-browser-app/theme-refs/wqdb-original.png (full page screenshot)

## Clint's directive
- Use THIS (wqdb.org) as the WQDB database's theme — "Better if the wqdb.org for it's theme."

## Site design notes
- Header: white background, "WormsQuoteDatabase" wordmark (gray gradient text) with the
  pink Worms-game worm mascot on the left (see wqdb-header-strip.png).
- Nav bar: dark navy blue (~#003366-ish; sample from screenshot) with white links:
  Home | Latest | Top Rated | Bottom Rated | Browse | Random | Search | Add Quote,
  plus Login / Register on the right with icons.
- Content: "Browse" heading, sort line "Down [-] | [+] Up | Listing quotes from 1 to 30".
- Quote headers: #number as blue link, "- Rating N -", thumbs-down / thumbs-up / flag icons.
- Quote rows alternate white and light-gray backgrounds.
- Quote body: dark text on the row background.

## Logo
